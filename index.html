What we cover

Skills

01

Find the work worth automating

Not all work can or should be automated. Learn frameworks for finding and evaluating potential automations, and choosing what gets built.

02

Design solutions before building

Choose the right tool for the job, from spreadsheets to full AI agents. Learn to write design documents that account for the systems, workflows, and human input involved, so that AI produces quality output every time. 

03

Get systems talking to each other

Understand how systems expose their data to other software, and how to securely pass data between systems. Learn how to extract, clean, and organize data from different sources.

04

Direct AI to write code

Learn how to give instructions that are precise enough to get the right code, and how to test the output to catch and prevent errors.

05

Master the handoff

Learn how to properly document automations, set up error monitoring, and ensure your team can use these workflows without you.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code or use no-code tools?+

Nope! No coding experience is necessary to take the class. We’ll teach you everything you need to know to start building automations using Claude Code. When there’s code, Claude writes it and you verify the results — learning to direct and review it is the skill we’re teaching.

What’s the time commitment?+

This bootcamp was built for busy professionals. You’ll attend a weekly 90-minute live class in weeks 1–4, and will also spend about 2 hours outside of class each week on your own build. Plan for about 30 hours total over the 6 week period, including time spent on your final project.

What software will I need to participate?+

n8n and Claude, plus the systems your school already uses. Both are low-cost subscriptions on top of tuition, and we’ll send the setup list and current pricing once you’re registered.

Does this teach how to use or build AI in the classroom?+

No, and deliberately so. Our primary focus is on school operations. We cover some academic operations, but don’t touch instruction or classroom AI.

What about student data privacy?+

Covered in session two, before you build anything that touches student information. You’ll leave with a checklist for deciding what’s safe to automate — and several of the highest-value workflows in a school don’t touch student data at all.

Can my school send more than one person?+

Yes, and we’d encourage it! Mention it on your call if two or more people plan to join from your school, and we’ll provide a small discount.
